Study Tips for Medical Students

1. Active Recall > Re-reading

Close the book and test yourself. Use questions and flashcards daily.

2. Spaced Repetition

Review material at increasing intervals (Anki is perfect for this).

3. Integrate Clinical Early

Link basic science to real patients and cases from day one.

4. Practice Past Papers

Simulate exam conditions. Analyse why you got questions wrong.

5. Protect Sleep & Exercise

Memory consolidation happens during sleep. Move your body daily.

6. Teach Someone

Explaining a concept out loud is one of the best ways to master it.
